# KERNELDARWIN demo identity

## Style Prompt
An editorial scientific instrument on warm paper. Use the LAB's exact ivory, ink, vermilion, and system typography. The film shows recorded experimental evidence, not live optimization. Large questions frame authentic product screenshots. Motion stays restrained: brief opacity/position entrances, clean crossfades, long settled reading holds. Persistent evidence labels keep the scope visible.

## Colors
- #f5f3ed: paper canvas
- #fffefa: instrument panels
- #242724: ink
- #c63f27: evidence accent
- #686c63: secondary annotations

## Typography
- Helvetica Neue / system sans: questions and product statements, heavy display weight
- SFMono-Regular / Consolas / monospace: evidence labels, units and repo identifier

## What NOT to Do
- No fake GPU activity, numerical results or copied competitor visuals.
- No glowing ambient dashboard, generic cards, particles or decorative charts.
- No re-created metrics; screenshots are from actual recorded CPU controls.
- No voice, music or SFX: this is an intentionally silent technical walkthrough.
